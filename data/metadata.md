**Census Data**: Census data is from the American Community Survey and retrieved from [data.census.gov](data.census.gov). On 10/17/2026, Maya Schroder pulled median household income (B19013) for 2021-2024 for all zip codes in California. 

**CFP Data**: CFP data is from the California Fair Plan Insurance Property Insurance and retreived from [cfpnet.com](https://www.cfpnet.com/key-statistics-data). On 10/18/2026, Matt Swire pulled pdfs containing previous 5 years of insurance data. 

**Fire Data**: Cal Fire data is from California Fire Department and retreived from [data.ca.gov](https://data.ca.gov/@cal-fire/california-fire-perimeters-1950). On 10/18/2026, Matt Swire pulled the historical fire data from 1950s onwards.
